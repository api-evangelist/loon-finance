# Loon and CADC

> CADC is a fiat-backed stablecoin pegged 1:1 to the Canadian dollar. Reserves are held in segregated cash deposits at ATB Financial and attested monthly by an independent auditor. CADC is available on Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, and Solana.

Last updated: 2026-07-12
